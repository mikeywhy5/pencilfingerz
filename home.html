Trusted By

Notable Clients

Pencil Fingerz has created album artwork and branding for some of music’s biggest names, including T-Pain, Lil Wayne, Snoop Dogg, Cam’ron, Yelawolf, The Underachievers, and many more.

Beyond the music industry, his work spans storefronts, public spaces, civic landmarks, and large-scale installations. Pencil Fingerz has delivered high-impact, high-value work for businesses, municipalities, and institutions across British Columbia, including Old Yale Brewing Co., Kent’s Ice Cream Co., the City of Chilliwack, The Embassy, and Watson Elementary.

From album covers to city-scale murals, every project is built to be seen, remembered, and made to last.

No AI. Ever.

Every piece starts as a pencil in a real hand and ends the same way. AI art is easy to spot: flat, generic, and it repeats itself. Pencil Fingerz makes work no algorithm could ever produce.

About

One hand, every medium.

Pencil Fingerz is a multidisciplinary artist based in Chilliwack, B.C., specializing in large-scale, high-value murals and paintings, with custom tattoos, logo design, digital art, and hand-painted clothing on the side. Whether it's a twenty-foot wall or a two-inch tattoo, every project starts from the same sketchbook, the same real hand, and the same obsession with line work, texture, and story. No AI, no shortcuts. AI art is obvious the moment you see it, and it will never produce the kind of custom, one-of-one results you get from Pencil Fingerz.

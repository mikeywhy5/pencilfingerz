About

One hand, every medium.

Pencil Fingerz is a multidisciplinary artist based in Chilliwack, B.C., specializing in large-scale, high-value murals and paintings, with custom tattoos, logo design, digital art, and hand-painted clothing on the side. Whether it's a twenty-foot wall or a two-inch tattoo, every project starts from the same sketchbook, the same real hand, and the same obsession with line work, texture, and story. No AI, no shortcuts. AI art is obvious the moment you see it, and it will never produce the kind of custom, one-of-one results you get from Pencil Fingerz.
